Master’s thesis · CSSSA 2026

LLM activists in an agent-based model of nonviolent resistance

A hybrid simulation where LLM-guided activists plan campaigns against rule-based police, citizens and institutional pillars. I compared three open-weight 8B models under zero-shot and chain-of-thought prompting, 100 runs each, and validated outcomes against NAVCO historical data.

MesavLLMLlama 3.1Qwen3statsmodels
Finding
Chain-of-thought prompting recovers the tactical diversity that zero-shot prompting suppresses.
Finding
Campaign outcomes follow an “early cascade or bust” pattern.
Scale
Six model configurations, 600 simulated campaigns on Unity HPC.
